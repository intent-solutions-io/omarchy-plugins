# Typing adventure adversarial review reconciliation

> This is the reconciliation record for the Blueprint, not product approval. It records how specialist, thinker-canon, executive-council, and independent Grok findings changed the plan. Verbatim council and Grok artifacts remain in the persistent session directory named below.

## Outcome

The vision survives, but the build authorization is deliberately narrower. The current recommendation is to prove three original attention mechanics and one separate native-runtime path before funding a durable game. The intended product remains a downloadable, local-first desktop application with an optional thin Omarchy companion. Gameplay, learner profiles, progress, and valid paid entitlements work locally. No hosted game runtime is planned.

The references supplied by the owner are quality signals, not brands to reproduce. The proof must test instant stakes, escalation, meaningful agency, visible world transformation, replay value, Omarchy craft, and parent-perceived value. It must not copy names, characters, likenesses, trade dress, thumbnail grammar, platform identity, or implied endorsement.

## Product and commercial boundary after review

The free product is a coherent beginner adventure, not a trial. Paid Family remains conditional and may add additional worlds, profiles, advanced punctuation and symbols, sustained fluency, code-like patterns, expanded parent views, and deeper adaptation. Safety, correction, accessibility, privacy controls, export, deletion, and the complete beginner path cannot be paywalled.

The game itself does not require an account, cloud profile, or server. A later purchase occurs in a parent-controlled external flow. The installed application receives only a signed entitlement without child data, payment credentials, or a password. A valid local purchase continues to work during vendor, network, or Intent Solutions service outages. The unavoidable refund-versus-permanent-offline-access tradeoff remains an owner decision that must be disclosed before sale.

## Audience and attention decision

The product should be teen-capable, not teen-only, and should avoid a babyish visual language. That is a test direction, not a target-market decision. Roblox's current disclosures justify testing an older-feeling treatment but do not establish that its users want a typing game or that their spending transfers to a parent purchase.

The experiment separates three questions:

1. Does the child immediately understand what is at stake and what typing changes?
2. Does the child voluntarily continue or return without pressure, streak loss, random rewards, or facilitator prompting?
3. Does a parent understand the permanent free value and choose a meaningful follow-up action for a hypothetical paid addition?

Failure on one question cannot be hidden by success on another.

## Governance and dependency corrections

- The master Beads epic now states that current authorization is planning and bounded proof only after the Wave 0 owner gate.
- Successor phase epics carry explicit blocking dependencies, and `bd dep cycles` reports no cycles.
- Human game, learning, research, accessibility, privacy, and data roles block minor-facing work.
- The research protocol is designed before recruitment; recruitment and study execution remain separate.
- Parent service deployment, entitlement signing, commerce, and publication remain behind later gates.
- Signing keys and entitlement authority cannot share the public intake host merely for convenience.
- The claim registry prohibits unsupported AI, audience, endorsement, learning, accessibility, privacy, reliability, and compliance claims.
- Model-specific project agents may prepare and audit work but cannot supply human legal, ethics, safeguarding, learning, accessibility, residual-risk, spending, or publication decisions.

## Review limitations and preserved dissent

The council broadly converged on bounded discovery but differed on emphasis. CMO required expressive spectacle in the proof. CFO required economics and service costs to remain outside the durable build until demand evidence exists. GC and CISO treated identity, minors, claims, rights, and trust-zone boundaries as non-negotiable. VP DevRel warned that a companion perceived as the game or as a downloader would damage community trust. CTO required replaceable presentation and content adapters around a deterministic learning core. CSO warned against declaring an AI creator platform before one differentiated mechanic works.

These positions are complementary constraints, not a manufactured unanimous product approval. Jeremy Longshore remains head of board. Public name, primary cohort, budget, repository creation, human appointments, exact prototype authorization, provider, price, hosting commitment, AI or creation expansion, public claims, and release remain owner-held.
