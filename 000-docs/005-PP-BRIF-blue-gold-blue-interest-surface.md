# BLUE GOLD BLUE Interest Surface Brief

## Purpose

Publish an honest research page at `/bluegoldblue/` for people asking about the BLUE GOLD BLUE concept. The page must explain the safety sequence, collect bounded product-interest signals, and avoid implying that a product, price, compatibility promise, partnership, or release date already exists.

## Audience and Action

The primary audience is an adult Windows user considering a future move to a supported open-source environment. The single primary action is to register interest and confirm an email address. Registration is research participation, not a purchase, reservation, waitlist position, or eligibility decision.

## Direction

Extend the established OMA portfolio system: restrained light surfaces, IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono, deep blue structure, and gold only for safety-critical emphasis. The first viewport must make the sequence visible: BLUE saves and verifies, a safety gate must pass, GOLD changes the computer, and BLUE restores supported data.

The page must remain useful without fabricated product renders, testimonials, prices, countdowns, availability claims, or Omarchy endorsement language.

## FAQ Contract

The FAQ is the progressive-disclosure layer for questions that should not overload the first viewport. Organize it by the visitor's actual concern: the basic kit and steps, computer compatibility and Windows consequences, files/apps/accounts, safety/privacy, and practical next steps. Each answer must be short, plain, and explicitly labeled when it describes a current fact, planned behavior, known limit, undecided offer term, or future idea.

The FAQ must directly answer destructive consequences, what happens when each physical device is connected, GOLD-first refusal, the no-current-product and no-DIY-media boundary, end-to-end proof status, full-system-backup limits, unsupported source computers and destination systems, pre-purchase checking and preview limits, Windows activation, unsupported programs, cloud-only files, credentials, Windows security prompts, critical-item review, destination familiarity, peripherals, Windows encryption and firmware checks, multi-drive boundaries, interruption during protection, installation, and restore, capacity, encryption and recovery, independent recovery without the product service, independent-backup requirements, migration/device binding and reuse, malware, STOP/REVIEW behavior, lack of a proven Windows rollback, preparation, restore reporting, failure recourse, time, support and helper privacy boundaries, accessibility, ongoing backup, WELCOME and AI data access, recurring-fee uncertainty, release-specific compatibility, pricing, fulfillment, website analytics/form data handling, and interest-registration boundaries. Unknowns stay unknown. A comprehensive FAQ is not evidence of product readiness.

On narrow mobile screens, preserve a visible route to the interest form in the header. The FAQ topic map may stack, but every disclosure must retain its status label, question, answer, keyboard behavior, and readable line length.

## Form Contract

Collect only name, email, one preferred offer concept, and bounded optional signals for journey, current Windows version, storage preference, estimated capacity, and timing. Require the current consent statement. Do not accept free text, files, passwords, license keys, health details, financial information, machine serial numbers, or customer payloads.

The verified backend must validate every enum, suppress honeypot submissions, rate-limit requests, encrypt pending records, and keep that sealed payload server-side. Email links carry only a 22-character base64url code whose digest maps to the 48-hour type-bound confirmation record. Confirmation is explicit and idempotent, already-issued token links remain compatible through their original expiry, and confirmed evidence survives CRM delivery failure.
