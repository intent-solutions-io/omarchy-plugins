# BLUE GOLD BLUE Interest Surface Brief

## Purpose

Publish an honest research page at `/bluegoldblue/` for people asking about the BLUE GOLD BLUE concept. The page must explain the safety sequence, collect bounded product-interest signals, and avoid implying that a product, price, compatibility promise, partnership, or release date already exists.

## Audience and Action

The primary audience is an adult Windows user considering a future move to a supported open-source environment. The single primary action is to register interest and confirm an email address. Registration is research participation, not a purchase, reservation, waitlist position, or eligibility decision.

## Direction

Extend the established OMA portfolio system: restrained light surfaces, IBM Plex Sans and JetBrains Mono, deep blue structure, and gold only for safety-critical emphasis. The first viewport must make the sequence visible: BLUE saves and verifies, a safety gate must pass, GOLD changes the computer, and BLUE restores supported data.

The page must remain useful without fabricated product renders, testimonials, prices, countdowns, availability claims, or Omarchy endorsement language.

## Form Contract

Collect only name, email, one preferred offer concept, and bounded optional signals for journey, current Windows version, storage preference, estimated capacity, and timing. Require the current consent statement. Do not accept free text, files, passwords, license keys, health details, financial information, machine serial numbers, or customer payloads.

The verified backend must validate every enum, suppress honeypot submissions, rate-limit requests, encrypt pending records, and keep that sealed payload server-side. Email links carry only a short opaque code whose digest maps to the 48-hour type-bound confirmation record. Confirmation is explicit and idempotent, already-issued token links remain compatible through their original expiry, and confirmed evidence survives CRM delivery failure.
