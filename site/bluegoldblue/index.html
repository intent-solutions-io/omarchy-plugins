Questions before you plug anything in

What you should know.

These answers separate the product experience we are working toward from what has already been proven. If a safety answer is not known, BLUE must stop instead of guessing.

TodayCan I buy BLUE GOLD BLUE now?

No. It is still in research and development. Registering interest only confirms that you want updates, research invitations, or possible pilot information. It does not place an order, reserve a kit, or promise a release date.

ImportantWill GOLD remove Windows from my computer?

On the same-computer journey, yes: the intended GOLD installation can replace Windows and erase the existing system disk. That is why GOLD must remain locked out until BLUE has independently verified the required files and the computer has passed compatibility checks. A future new-computer journey could leave the old Windows computer untouched.

PlannedWhat will BLUE save?

The first version is intended to discover and protect supported personal files in known Windows locations, then produce a clear record of what was found, copied, skipped, or needs attention. We will not claim that it saves "everything." Final file types, locations, exclusions, and size limits will be published only after testing.

Known limitWhat about OneDrive files that are only in the cloud?

A placeholder is not a protected copy. BLUE must either obtain and verify the actual file bytes or clearly report the item as unprotected and stop when it is required. The exact supported cloud-storage behavior is still being researched.

Known limitWill my Windows programs move to Omarchy?

Not automatically. Programs will be classified as native, web-based, replaceable with an alternative, or unsupported. If a program you consider essential has no acceptable path, the compatibility result should tell you to stop before GOLD.

Known limitWill passwords and signed-in sessions move?

Do not assume they will. Saved passwords, browser sessions, passkeys, license activations, and app credentials have different security rules. The conservative expectation is that you will sign in again, and BLUE will not expose secrets just to make the transition appear easier.

PlannedWill this work on my computer?

The initial research target is one primary user on a personal Windows 10 or 11 computer with explicitly supported x86-64 hardware. A final compatibility list does not exist yet. Work-managed computers, shared multi-user systems, older Windows versions, unusual storage setups, and unsupported hardware may be excluded or require a different path.

Safety ruleWhat does "safe" mean?

It must mean more than a completed progress bar. BLUE is intended to compare the discovered inventory with the protected copy, verify file content independently, confirm required capacity and compatibility, and preserve evidence. Missing, stale, or conflicting evidence must produce STOP or REVIEW, never an all-clear.

Safety ruleHow will GOLD avoid erasing the wrong drive?

The installation design requires positive destination identification, clear device details, protection of BLUE and removable media, and a final authorization gate. Those controls are not yet proven on real customer hardware. Until they are, GOLD is not ready for customer use.

Still provingWhat if the copy fails, power goes out, or I unplug BLUE?

The intended behavior is to stop without declaring success, preserve an understandable record, and support a safe retry. Exact interruption, resume, and recovery behavior still needs fault-injection testing. You should never be told to continue to GOLD after an incomplete or uncertain protection step.

Current factIs BLUE an internet cloud, and are my files uploaded?

No. BLUE means a physical local-storage device that stays with you. The core migration should not require uploading your personal files to Intent Solutions. Local storage alone is not remote backup, however, and any future network service would require separate, explicit disclosure and consent.

UndecidedWhich BLUE size should I choose?

256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, and 2 TB are capacity candidates, not confirmed products or tiers. BLUE must have enough usable space for the files it promises to protect, with room for verification data and safe operation. Qualification should recommend a fit only after measuring your actual supported data.

UndecidedWhat if BLUE is lost, stolen, or damaged?

The encryption, recovery, replacement, and key-management experience is not final. A local device can be lost or fail, so BLUE should not be described as permanent or disaster-proof storage. These risks must be resolved before a commercial offer.

Known limitCan BLUE put Windows back if I change my mind?

That is not currently promised. BLUE is being designed to protect and restore supported personal data, not to create a complete Windows system image with every program and setting. The future new-computer path is the clearest way to keep the original Windows machine intact.

UndecidedWill I need internet access, technical skill, or a lot of downtime?

The goal is a guided plug-in experience that does not require Linux or backup expertise. Exact internet requirements, completion times, support boundaries, and novice usability have not been proven. Data size, computer speed, downloads, and exceptions will affect timing.

Future ideaWhat happens to BLUE after the move?

BLUE could become an ongoing local backup device, but that is a future concept rather than a current promise. WELCOME, an optional local assistant, could later help people find files and learn Omarchy. It would never decide that data is safe or authorize a destructive step.

UndecidedHow much will it cost, when will it ship, and what support will I get?

Pricing, final offers, shipping regions, warranties, returns, support levels, and release timing are not set. The options on this page are demand-research concepts. Confirmed subscribers will receive updates when evidence supports a real pilot or offer.

Current factIs Omarchy involved in this project?

No. BLUE GOLD BLUE is an independent Intent Solutions research project. It is not an Omarchy endorsement, partnership, official migration product, or current offer to distribute Omarchy media. Any future distribution would require the proper technical and legal permissions.

Out of scopeCan BLUE rescue a computer that is already failing?

Do not rely on this research project as a rescue service. A failing drive or unstable computer needs a separate recovery approach, and continued use can make data loss worse. The first product scope is a controlled migration from a working, supported computer.
