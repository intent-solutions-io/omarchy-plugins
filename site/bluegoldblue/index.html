Three steps. That is the whole idea.

We are programming the complicated parts into BLUE and GOLD so the customer should not have to learn backup tools, installation media, or Linux disk names.

  1. 1
    BLUE. Save your stuff.

    Plug the personal cloud into Windows. BLUE finds supported files, checks that the device has enough room, copies the files, and verifies the copy.

  2. 2
    GOLD. Start Omarchy.

    After BLUE gives the all-clear, remove it and plug in the GOLD key. GOLD starts the supported Omarchy installation process.

  3. 3
    BLUE. Bring your stuff home.

    When Omarchy starts, plug BLUE in again. BLUE restores supported files and explains anything that still needs attention.

The details, limits, and current proof.

The simple customer model is the goal. The project underneath it is currently proving read-only Windows discovery, migration contracts, safety controls, synthetic fixtures, and the rule that BLUE must come before GOLD.

  • Initial focus: one personal Windows 10 or 11 user on explicitly supported x86-64 hardware.
  • Safety rule: no “your stuff is safe” result without independent verification.
  • BLUE means a physical local-storage device. It is not an internet cloud service and does not currently provide remote redundancy.
  • Omarchy is the first destination under evaluation. Supported versions and computers are not final.

What is not decided.

Final device hardware, capacities, pricing, fulfillment, support, computer compatibility, release timing, ongoing backup, WELCOME capabilities, and commercial offers remain open until research and testing provide evidence.
