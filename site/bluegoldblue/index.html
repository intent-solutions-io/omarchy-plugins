Three steps for the customer. Serious proof underneath.

The idea is deliberately simple on the outside. The engineering work is deliberately conservative underneath, because a migration tool must earn the right to change a computer.

  1. 1
    BLUE saves first.

    Find the supported personal data, measure what is there, copy it, verify it, and record exactly what happened.

  2. 2
    GOLD stays locked behind proof.

    The new-computer step must not proceed when protection, identity, capacity, health, or compatibility evidence is missing.

  3. 3
    BLUE brings supported data back.

    Restore the supported digital life, explain exceptions honestly, and help the new environment feel recognizable.

What exists today.

The project has a canonical architecture, migration contracts, risk controls, synthetic fixtures, and bounded prototype work for read-only Windows discovery. The current work is evidence gathering, not a consumer release.

  • Initial focus: one personal Windows 10 or 11 user on explicitly supported x86-64 hardware.
  • Safety rule: no “your stuff is safe” result without independent verification.
  • Destination: upstream Omarchy is the first environment under evaluation, not a permanent lock-in.

What is still unknown.

Final BLUE media, device capacities, pricing, fulfillment, support model, supported computer list, release timing, and every commercial offer remain open until experiments provide evidence.

This is an independent Intent Solutions project. It is not an Omarchy endorsement, official migration product, or offer to distribute Omarchy media.
