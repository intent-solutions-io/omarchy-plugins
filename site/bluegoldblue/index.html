Start here

The basic idea, what would arrive, and what you would do.

Plain answerWhat exactly is BLUE GOLD BLUE?

It is a product journey we are researching for people who want to move from Windows to Omarchy without becoming backup or Linux experts. BLUE is a physical personal cloud that saves and verifies supported files. GOLD is the installation key. Then BLUE brings the supported files back. It is not a finished product yet.

Plain answerWhat is Omarchy?

Omarchy is the Linux-based computer environment being evaluated as the first destination for this project. On the same-computer journey, it would become your main environment in place of Windows. Some familiar Windows programs may not run there, so application compatibility has to be checked before any switch.

Leading ideaWhat would arrive at my door?

The leading idea is a prepared BLUE storage device and a separate GOLD installation key, with very simple instructions. BLUE would be yours to keep. The exact devices, packaging, contents, shipping method, and whether every offer includes both devices are not final.

Leading ideaDo I send you my computer?

Not for the basic kit being considered. The intended work happens on your computer with the devices in your hands. Assisted service and preconfigured-computer options are separate ideas, and their shipping or handling process has not been defined.

Three stepsWhat would I actually have to do?

Plug BLUE into Windows and choose to save your stuff. Wait for BLUE to verify the copy and check the computer. Only after an all-clear would you remove BLUE, insert GOLD, and start the supported Omarchy setup. When Omarchy starts, plug BLUE in again to restore supported files. Some computers may still need machine-specific startup instructions.

TodayCan I buy BLUE GOLD BLUE now?

No. It is still in research and development. Registering interest only confirms that you want updates, research invitations, or possible pilot information. It does not place an order, reserve a kit, charge you, or promise a release date.

Current factIs Omarchy involved in this project?

No. BLUE GOLD BLUE is an independent Intent Solutions research project. It is not an Omarchy endorsement, partnership, official migration product, or current offer to distribute Omarchy media. Any future distribution would require the proper technical and legal permissions.

Your computer and the switch

Which machines are being considered and what happens to Windows.

Research targetWill this work on my computer?

The initial research target is one primary user on a personal Windows 10 or 11 computer with explicitly supported Intel or AMD 64-bit hardware and a straightforward internal drive. A final compatibility list does not exist. BLUE must check the actual computer and stop when required evidence is missing or unsupported.

Not in V1What about Macs, Linux computers, older Windows versions, or ARM PCs?

They are not part of the first product boundary. The design may support more source computers later, but the first proof is intentionally narrow: supported Windows 10 or 11 computers using Intel or AMD 64-bit processors.

Known limitWhat about a work computer, shared family computer, or several Windows users?

The first target is a personal, non-company-managed computer with one primary user. Work-managed devices, shared systems, and multiple user profiles add permissions, ownership, policy, and privacy questions. They may be excluded or require a separate process.

Two journeysDo I switch this computer or move to a new one?

Both journeys are being considered. The same-computer path would replace Windows after BLUE passes every safety gate. The new-computer path would save from the old computer and restore onto a separately qualified computer, leaving the old machine intact as a fallback. The new-computer option is a future product path, not a current offer.

ImportantWill GOLD remove Windows from my computer?

On the same-computer journey, yes. The intended GOLD installation can replace Windows and erase the existing system disk. That is why GOLD must remain locked out until BLUE has independently verified the required files and the computer has passed compatibility checks.

Not promisedWill this create a Windows and Omarchy dual-boot computer?

Do not expect dual boot. The simple same-computer journey is being designed as a controlled replacement, not a menu that keeps two operating systems side by side. A new-computer journey is the safer concept for someone who wants to keep the original Windows computer available.

Known limitCan BLUE put Windows back if I change my mind?

That is not currently promised. BLUE is being designed to protect and restore supported personal data, not to create a complete Windows system image with every program, license, and setting. Reinstalling Windows would be a separate recovery process.

Out of scopeCan BLUE rescue a computer that is already failing?

Do not rely on this research project as a rescue service. A failing drive or unstable computer needs a separate recovery approach, and continued use can make data loss worse. The first product scope is a controlled migration from a working, supported computer.

Your files, apps, and accounts

What should move automatically, what needs another app, and what may need attention.

PlannedWhat will BLUE save?

The first version is intended to discover and protect supported personal files in known Windows locations, then produce a clear record of what was found, copied, skipped, or needs attention. We will not claim that it saves everything. Final file types, locations, exclusions, and size limits will be published only after testing.

Known limitWhat about OneDrive, Dropbox, or files that are only online?

A cloud placeholder is not a protected copy. BLUE must either obtain and verify the actual file or clearly report it as unprotected. If that file is required, BLUE must stop. Exact support for each cloud-storage service is still being researched.

Known limitWill my Windows programs move to Omarchy?

Not automatically. BLUE may identify programs and classify the likely path as a native Omarchy app, a web app, an alternative, or unsupported. It will not copy Windows program files and pretend they will work. If an essential program has no acceptable path, the result should tell you to stop before GOLD.

Check firstWhat about Microsoft Office, Adobe apps, QuickBooks, games, or other must-have software?

Each important program needs a truthful compatibility answer before you switch. Some may have web versions, Linux versions, or acceptable alternatives. Others may not work or may need a new license or sign-in. BLUE should ask what is essential and produce REVIEW or STOP when the answer is uncertain or unacceptable.

Check firstWill my photos and documents still open?

Restoring a file and being able to use it are related but different questions. Common formats may open in native or alternative apps, but unusual, proprietary, damaged, or application-specific files may not. Compatibility testing must cover both the file copy and the destination app needed to use it.

Known limitWill passwords and signed-in sessions move?

Do not assume they will. Saved passwords, browser sessions, passkeys, license activations, and app credentials have different security rules. The conservative expectation is that you will sign in again. BLUE will not expose or copy secrets merely to make the move look easier.

PlannedWhat about browser bookmarks, email, contacts, and calendars?

Bookmarks and other safe familiarity data may be translated when a supported path is proven. Email, contacts, and calendars often return after you sign in to the original service. Locally stored mail archives or unusual browser profiles need explicit support and cannot be assumed. Account secrets and live sessions remain excluded unless a separate safe policy is approved.

Report clearlyWhat if a file is locked, encrypted, damaged, hidden, or in an unusual place?

BLUE should never silently skip it and still say everything is safe. Supported exceptions must be listed with a plain-language reason. Important unreadable or unavailable content must produce REVIEW or STOP until it is recovered, deliberately excluded, or handled through an approved process.

Check firstWhat about printers, scanners, webcams, Bluetooth devices, or special hardware?

BLUE is intended to inventory important devices and check whether the destination has a supported path. Support will vary by exact model and may require testing. If a device you depend on is unsupported or unknown, the compatibility result should tell you before GOLD is allowed to proceed.

Safety and privacy

How the design is supposed to fail safely when something is wrong or unknown.

Safety ruleWhat does “safe” mean?

It must mean more than a completed progress bar. BLUE is intended to compare what it discovered with the protected copy, verify file content independently, confirm required capacity and compatibility, and preserve a record. Missing, stale, or conflicting evidence must produce STOP or REVIEW, never an all-clear.

Safety ruleHow will GOLD avoid erasing the wrong drive?

The installation design requires positive computer and destination identification, clear device details, protection of BLUE and other removable media, and a final authorization check. Those controls are not yet proven on real customer hardware. Until they are, GOLD is not ready for customer use.

Still provingWhat if the copy fails, power goes out, or I unplug BLUE?

The intended behavior is to stop without declaring success, keep an understandable record, and support a safe retry. Exact interruption, resume, and recovery behavior still needs fault-injection testing. You should never be told to continue to GOLD after an incomplete or uncertain protection step.

UndecidedWhat if BLUE is lost, stolen, or damaged?

A local device can be lost or fail, so BLUE must not be described as permanent or disaster-proof storage. Encryption, recovery copies, replacement, and failure handling are still being proven. Losing BLUE after Windows has been replaced could be serious, which is why the commercial design cannot move ahead without a tested recovery story.

PlannedWill BLUE be encrypted, and what happens if I lose the recovery key?

The current research direction encrypts file contents and filenames while keeping the format independently recoverable with open tools. Private recovery material would stay separate from BLUE. The customer experience and key-recovery process are not final. If every recovery copy is lost, encrypted data may be permanently unrecoverable.

Current factIs BLUE an internet cloud, and are my files uploaded?

No. BLUE means a physical local-storage device that stays with you. The core migration should not require uploading your personal files to Intent Solutions. Local storage alone is not remote backup, however, and any future network service would require separate, explicit disclosure and consent.

Still provingCould BLUE copy malware or dangerous files?

Migration must treat saved content as untrusted. The restore design must not automatically run migrated programs, scripts, or unknown executables. Malware scanning and customer guidance still need a defined, tested policy, so BLUE cannot promise that every saved file is harmless.

Safety ruleWhat if BLUE does not have enough room?

BLUE must measure supported data and usable capacity before it claims protection. If there is not enough room, it should stop and explain what is needed. It must not quietly leave required files behind or approve GOLD based on a partial copy.

Safety ruleWhat happens if BLUE says REVIEW or STOP?

Do not use GOLD. REVIEW means something needs a clear answer or guided decision. STOP means the current plan is not safe or supported. Your Windows computer and BLUE should remain unchanged while you retry, get help, choose a different computer, or decide not to continue.

Time, help, and what comes next

The practical questions about using the kit, getting help, and keeping BLUE.

Design goalDo I need to know Linux, use a terminal, or understand computer drives?

That is exactly what the product is meant to avoid. The normal journey should use plain choices such as save my stuff and bring my stuff back. Some computers may still require a short startup step, but it should be specific to that machine. Novice usability has not yet been proven in customer trials.

UndecidedWill I need internet access?

Probably for some downloads, account sign-ins, updates, and application setup, but the exact requirement is not final. The core copy between your computer and BLUE should remain local. Any required online step must be disclosed before the switch, not discovered halfway through it.

Still provingHow long will the move take?

There is no honest universal estimate yet. Time will depend on how much data you have, the speed and health of the computer and BLUE device, downloads, installation, verification, and any exceptions. Qualification should provide a realistic estimate only after measuring the actual setup.

UndecidedWhat help will I get if I am stuck?

The support model is not set. The product is being designed for clear on-screen recovery instructions, and an assisted-migration option is under consideration. Hours, channels, response times, remote assistance, and what is included in each offer must be defined and tested before sale.

UndecidedWhich BLUE size should I choose?

256 GB, 512 GB, 1 TB, and 2 TB are capacity candidates, not confirmed products or tiers. BLUE must have enough usable space for the files it promises to protect, with room for verification data and safe operation. Qualification should recommend a fit only after measuring your actual supported data.

Possible optionCan I use my own storage drive or buy only the software?

A customer-supplied-drive or software-only path is being considered. It would need clear requirements because drive health, capacity, speed, formatting, encryption, and connection type all affect safety. It is not yet a confirmed offer.

Future ideaWhat happens to BLUE after the move?

You would keep the physical device. BLUE could later become an ongoing local backup device, but that is a future concept rather than a current promise. A migration copy also is not the same thing as a complete, off-site backup plan.

Future ideaWhat is WELCOME, and is it an AI assistant?

WELCOME is the optional onboarding step after restore. It could explain where files went, help with unfamiliar apps, and teach the new environment. A private local assistant is one future idea. It would be optional, would not be required for migration, and could never decide that your data is safe or authorize GOLD.

UndecidedHow much will it cost, where will it ship, and what about warranties or returns?

Pricing, final offers, shipping regions, taxes, warranties, returns, replacements, and release timing are not set. The options on this page are demand-research concepts. Those terms must be published clearly before anyone is asked to buy.
